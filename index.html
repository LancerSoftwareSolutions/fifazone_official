Our process

Every card, verified before it reaches you.

  1. 01

    Submit

    Cards arrive at our facility from vetted suppliers and consigning collectors, logged against their claimed set and print run.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    A specialist checks centering, surface, corners and edges under raking light, and cross-references the serial number against the issuer's records.

  3. 03

    Grade & seal

    Approved cards are graded, logged with a public certificate number, and sealed in a tamper-evident case.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Every order goes out insured and tracked, packed rigid to survive transit without a single bent corner.
